How Learn works
  1. New terms are asked with your first question type (multiple choice by default). Right answer moves them to Familiar.
  2. Familiar terms come back as your next type (written by default). Right answer makes them Mastered.
  3. Miss one and it stays where it is and comes back later in the same round.
  4. Rounds pull Familiar terms first, then new ones. You finish when every term is Mastered.
