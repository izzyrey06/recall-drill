How Learn works
  1. New terms are asked as multiple choice. Right answer moves them to Familiar.
  2. Familiar terms come back as a written answer. Right answer makes them Mastered.
  3. Miss one and it drops a level and comes back later in the same round.
  4. Rounds pull Familiar terms first, then new ones. You finish when every term is Mastered.
